# Roadmap

This page consolidates all upcoming features and platform infrastructure changes across every area of watsonx Orchestrate. It is synthesised from the Deep Dive Enablement sessions and reflects commitments and plans expressed at the time of those sessions.

!!! warning "Roadmap accuracy"
    Roadmap items reflect information from the IBM watsonx Orchestrate Deep Dive Enablement sessions. Timelines and feature scope may have changed. Always verify current roadmap status with your IBM account team or the official IBM release notes before making commitments to customers.
